SECTION 1 — CARDIAC DIAGNOSTIC IMAGING
---------------------------------------
Policy Rule ID  : ACC-901
Procedure Group : Advanced Cardiac Diagnostic Imaging
Covered Service : Cardiac Magnetic Resonance Imaging (Cardiac MRI), Cardiac CT
                  Angiography, Myocardial Perfusion Imaging

COVERAGE CRITERIA (ALL conditions must be met simultaneously):
  [C1] Patient must have a documented clinical diagnosis of ATYPICAL CHEST PAIN
       recorded by a licensed cardiologist or attending physician in the medical
       chart prior to the date of service.
  [C2] A resting Electrocardiogram (EKG / ECG) performed within 90 days of the
       requested service must show one or more ABNORMAL findings, including but
       not limited to: ST-segment depression or elevation, T-wave inversion,
       left ventricular hypertrophy (LVH), bundle branch block, pathological Q
       waves, sinus bradycardia, or other rhythm/conduction abnormalities.

NON-COVERAGE TRIGGERS:
  - Normal resting EKG with no documented arrhythmia or conduction defect.
  - Chest pain classified as TYPICAL (musculoskeletal or GERD-related) without
    cardiologist attestation of atypical origin.
  - Absence of prior resting EKG result in the patient's clinical record.

DENIAL CODE : ERR-CARDIAC
APPEAL BASIS : Submit updated EKG report with cardiologist attestation letter
               confirming atypical chest pain classification. Peer-to-peer review
               with a board-certified cardiologist may be requested.

SECTION 2 — ORTHOPAEDIC SURGICAL PROCEDURES
--------------------------------------------
Policy Rule ID  : ORTHO-404
Procedure Group : Arthroscopic Surgical Interventions
Covered Service : Arthroscopic procedures of the knee (meniscectomy, ACL repair,
                  chondroplasty), hip (labral repair, femoroacetabular impingement
                  correction), and shoulder (rotator cuff repair, SLAP repair,
                  subacromial decompression)

COVERAGE CRITERIA (ALL conditions must be met simultaneously):
  [C1] Patient must have completed a MINIMUM of FOUR (4) CONSECUTIVE CALENDAR
       MONTHS of conservative, clinically supervised therapy. Qualifying therapies
       include: formal Physical Therapy (PT) sessions with a licensed physiotherapist
       OR documented intra-articular corticosteroid / hyaluronic acid injections
       administered by a physician.
  [C2] Conservative therapy participation must be CONTINUOUSLY DOCUMENTED in the
       official Electronic Health Record (EHR) system. Home exercise programs,
       informal stretching, yoga, acupuncture, or over-the-counter NSAID use
       without formal clinical documentation do NOT satisfy this requirement.
  [C3] Imaging evidence (X-Ray, MRI, or CT scan) must confirm structural pathology
       consistent with the proposed surgical intervention.

NON-COVERAGE TRIGGERS:
  - Conservative therapy duration is less than 4 continuous months.
  - Documentation gaps or reliance solely on home/informal remedies.
  - Lack of imaging confirming structural pathology.

DENIAL CODE : ERR-ORTHO
APPEAL BASIS : Provide complete physiotherapy attendance logs, physician notes
               documenting clinical injections, and updated imaging reports. An
               independent medical examination (IME) may be ordered.

SECTION 3 — ONCOLOGY TARGETED THERAPY & IMMUNOTHERAPY
------------------------------------------------------
Policy Rule ID  : ONCO-202
Procedure Group : Targeted Molecular Therapy & Immune Checkpoint Inhibitor Therapy
Covered Service : All FDA-approved targeted therapy agents (e.g., Trastuzumab,
                  Cetuximab, Vemurafenib, Osimertinib) and immune checkpoint
                  inhibitors (e.g., Pembrolizumab, Nivolumab, Atezolizumab)

COVERAGE CRITERIA (ALL conditions must be met simultaneously):
  [C1] A COMPANION DIAGNOSTIC GENETIC TEST must have been performed on a biopsy
       sample from the patient's primary or metastatic tumour. Acceptable tests
       include: Next-Generation Sequencing (NGS) panels, Fluorescence In Situ
       Hybridization (FISH), Immunohistochemistry (IHC), or polymerase chain
       reaction (PCR)-based mutation assays.
  [C2] The test result must CONFIRM THE PRESENCE of the SPECIFIC BIOMARKER
       MUTATION or expression level for which the requested agent is indicated.
       Examples:
         - Pembrolizumab / Nivolumab : PD-L1 expression score >= 1% (TPS or CPS);
           high TMB (>= 10 mut/Mb) also qualifies.
         - Trastuzumab               : HER2-POSITIVE status (IHC 3+ or FISH ratio >= 2.0).
         - Cetuximab / Panitumumab   : RAS/KRAS/NRAS WILD-TYPE (mutation-FREE) confirmed.
         - Vemurafenib / Dabrafenib  : BRAF V600E/K mutation CONFIRMED.
         - Osimertinib               : EGFR exon 19 deletion or L858R mutation CONFIRMED.
  [C3] The oncology treatment plan must be co-signed by a board-certified medical
       oncologist and the treatment must be consistent with NCCN Clinical Practice
       Guidelines (Category 1 or 2A evidence).

NON-COVERAGE TRIGGERS:
  - No companion diagnostic test on file prior to treatment initiation.
  - Biomarker test result is NEGATIVE or ABSENT for the targeted agent's required
    marker (e.g., HER2-negative for Trastuzumab; KRAS-mutated for Cetuximab;
    PD-L1 < 1% for Pembrolizumab without TMB qualification).
  - Use of an agent outside its FDA-approved biomarker indication.

DENIAL CODE : ERR-ONCOLOGY
APPEAL BASIS : Submit full pathology report with companion diagnostic results,
               oncologist co-signature, and NCCN guideline citation supporting
               off-label or alternate-biomarker use. Tumour Board minutes may
               be submitted as supplemental clinical evidence.
